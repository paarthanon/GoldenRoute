The Core Ranking Formula

Total Time to Definitive Care (TTDC)

Stage 1: The Hard Capability Gate (Binary Filter)
Eligible = ∏_{k ∈ R} E_k
Where R is the set of required life-support equipment IDs selected by the clinic, and E_k ∈ {0, 1} indicates whether equipment k is operational and available. If Eligible = 0, the facility is disqualified from active destination ranking.
Stage 2: The Additive Time Score (S)
S = T_transit + ΔT_dock + ΔT_prep + P_surge
Optimal Destination = arg min(S)
Sort order: Ascending by TTDC Score (min S = Rank #1 Recommended Match).
Dynamic Bed-Hold Duration Calculation
Bed Hold Duration (Minutes) = T_transit + T_buffer
Where T_transit is live road travel time and T_buffer is clinical intake staging buffer (set to 10 minutes). For Max Hospital: 14m + 10m = 24 minutes.
T_transit — Road Travel Time Traffic Telemetry

Driving duration in minutes considering live traffic along the transit corridor.

ΔT_dock — Emergency Dock Handover Delay N_queue × 7 mins

Calculated as N_queue × t_triage, where t_triage = 7 mins standard handover lag per queued ambulance. (e.g. BSA has 4 queued = +28m delay).

ΔT_prep — Bed & Equipment Staging Lag 0m to 10m

0 mins if designated bay is pre-sanitized and calibrated; +5 to +10 mins if terminal disinfection or machine calibration is required.

P_surge — Capacity Saturation Risk Penalty V_free Step Function

Virtual penalty to prevent routing inbound cases to near-exhaustion facilities:
• V_free ≥ 3: 0 mins penalty
• V_free = 2: +3 mins penalty
• V_free = 1: +8 mins penalty
